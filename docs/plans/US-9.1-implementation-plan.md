## Approach

The removal is a **net-diff-to-base** problem. Every notes hunk was added on top of `6546db8`, and for five of the seven files it
touches the notes were the ONLY change US-9.1 made, so removal returns those files to their base bytes. Verified in this run
(`git diff 6546db8 HEAD`): `src/app/types.ts`, `src/app/app.component.ts`, `src/app/app.component.html` carry nothing but notes lines;
`src/services/content-orchestrator.service.ts` carries the notes lines plus the two FR-14 text lines (which stay). So the design
states, per file, "what the end state is" and gives each an independent mechanical oracle (`git diff 6546db8 -- <file>` is empty, or
equals the surviving hunks), instead of describing edits. The two FROZEN files are edited by exactly the two NFR-15 hunks, which are
the reverse of `7397081` (`task-a.ts`, 2 lines) and of `6db2885` (master, the two `[SOURCE FIDELITY]` bullets, 1095 chars become 965).
A sibling-file workaround is impossible: the text to remove lives in the frozen files. The golden `full-description-prompts.json`
cannot be `git revert`ed (`d5d207a` rewrote the same lines) and no tracked regeneration tool exists, so it is changed by a stated,
reviewable raw-text substitution (W6) **performed by the builder in T43, in the same commit as the master revert** (FR-43); TEST_WRITING
adds only an independent oracle pin for it, red on arrival. The tests of the withdrawn feature are removed or inverted by TEST_WRITING (not by the builder),
absence pins (FR-44) replace them, and one source-scan pin closes the coverage gaps the impact analysis lists. Delivery is forward
commits on the existing branch with no history rewrite (NFR-13).

## Rejected alternatives

1. **Sibling-file approach for the frozen hunks.** Cannot remove text that lives in the frozen files.
2. **Wholesale `git revert` of the eleven notes commits.** They are interleaved with surviving commits (for example `6db2885` also moved the golden that `d5d207a` then rewrote); conflicts, and it would also revert hunks (FR-11, FR-20) that stay.
3. **History rewrite / force-push dropping the notes commits.** Erases commits carrying recorded FROZEN grants and breaks the open draft PR #142; `so-pr-creator` never force-pushes (OD-23).
4. **Leave `ProductInput.operatorNotes` or `OperatorNotes` as a dormant type** to keep stored history typed. Dead code, contradicts the Story, and a type with no reader hides the FR-44 item 2 cast need.
5. **Regenerate the golden from actual builder output** (parse/stringify). Freezes any defect into the golden (R1) and may reformat the whole file; rejected for the raw-text substitution with an independent oracle (§7.7).
6. **First `AppComponent` TestBed spec** to prove restore/send/clear behaviour. Heavy DI, rejected at US-2.2; the scan, text pins and net-diff oracle are the proportionate substitute (R9).
7. **OD-21 option (b)** (keep the count-free wording naming three blocks). Would leave a second master text with no precedent and change the FR-42 literals and the golden oracle; not the owner's default.
8. **Keep the deleted specs as skipped tests** or weaken them. Forbidden (§7.7); NFR-14 removes them as a traced requirement withdrawal.
9. **Commit a golden regeneration tool.** New tooling the Story does not ask for; the procedure is recorded instead.
10. **Edit the Specification to fix its four inaccuracies from here.** Not this stage's act; none blocks the plan (see "Specification inaccuracies"); the readings travel to RECONCILIATION through the test artifacts instead.
11. **TEST_WRITING commits the regenerated golden (v5 Files list).** Turns `full-description.golden.spec.ts` red in 10 cases while the master is unmoved (a red that is not sanctioned), contradicts FR-43 "same commit as the master revert" and every earlier precedent (`6db2885`, `d5d207a`, `ecaa2fd`), and the TEST_WRITING commit `3f9ae29` never did it. Rejected for builder ownership with a RED-on-arrival oracle pin.
12. **Co-commit the inversions (P3, P4) with the master revert.** Impossible: TEST_WRITING precedes IMPLEMENTATION. Rejected for the per-task gate convention, stated to the human.
13. **Title-only edits of `description-metrics.spec.ts` and `master-system-prompt.liveliness.spec.ts`.** Touch tests of surviving requirements that NFR-14 does not list; the stale titles are harmless. Dropped (W7), or an explicit exception for the human.
