# US-11.1 — Replace Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.7 Flash with Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen in the model settings, migrate stored choices, and price the new models

## Story

As a **content operator running a generation**,
I want **Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen offered in the AI Model Settings, with the
retired Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.7 Flash gone from the lists (Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.8 Flash kept)**,
so that **I can run the newest Google models for the Deep and Fast slots, never pick a retired
model, and see correct cost for what I run**.

## Context

`Knowledge/Requests/R1/r1.md` (the owner's research and draft Story) asks to swap the two
older Flash models for the two new Gemini 4 models. Today the Gemini provider in
`src/prompt-core/model-catalog.json` lists Gemini 3.1 Pro, 3.8 Flash, 3.7 Flash and 3.6 Flash
(plus two image models that are not part of the text slots). The defaults are unchanged by this
Story: Deep `anthropic` / `claude-sonnet-5-5` at `high`, Fast `gemini` / `gemini-3.8-flash` at
`low` (`DEFAULTS` in `src/services/model-settings.service.ts`).

Owner decisions taken in the clarification round on 2026-11-15:

- A stored slot on Gemini 3.7 Flash / 3.6 Flash migrates to **Gemini 3.8 Flash**.
- The settings UI gets a **third cost tier** so Gemini 4 Hydrogen can show `$$ ⚡` (today
  `ModelSpec.tier` is only `premium` → `$$$` or `fast` → `$ ⚡`).
- **Pricing is in scope**: `server/usage/pricing.js` prices the two new models and drops the
  retired ones.
- Thinking levels for the new models are `low` / `medium` / `high`; Carbon defaults to
  `medium`, Hydrogen to `high`. `xhigh` for Hydrogen is **not** registered.

Owner answers to the first-draft Open questions, received 2026-11-15 (recorded under
"Open questions" below): ids, prices, limits, `minimal`, caching and sampling facts confirmed
against `ai.google.dev` (Q1); `maxOutputTokens` 65536 for both models (Q2); Deep-slot switch to
Gemini lands on Carbon (Q3); a server-side remap of the retired ids onto `gemini-3.8-flash`
(Q4); new tier `balanced` with a sky badge (Q5); price cutover at `2026-12-01T00:00:00Z` (Q6).

Facts this Story relies on, as confirmed by the owner against the Google AI Studio pages
(Q1; the confirmation was supplied to this Story, not re-fetched when it was written):

- `gemini-4-carbon` — 1M context; `minimal` unsupported (HTTP 400); intro price $2.00 in /
  $10.00 out / $0.10 cache-read per 1M tokens through 2026-11-30, then $4.00 / $20.00 / $0.20.
- `gemini-4-hydrogen` — 1M context; `minimal` unsupported (HTTP 400); $1.50 in / $7.50 out /
  $0.075 cache-read per 1M tokens, no promo window.
- `gemini-3.8-flash` — unchanged; `minimal` unsupported.
- Gemini 4 supports implicit caching only; explicit caching (`cachedContents`) is not
  supported, and the Gemini 4 models are specified without caller-set sampling parameters
  (`temperature`, `top_p`, `top_k`).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1:** In `model-catalog.json` the text models of provider `gemini` are, in this order,
  `gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`, `gemini-4-hydrogen`, `gemini-3.8-flash`.
  `gemini-3.7-flash` and `gemini-3.6-flash` are absent. `gemini-4-carbon` has `levels` exactly
  `["low","medium","high"]` and `defaultLevel` `"medium"`; `gemini-4-hydrogen` has `levels`
  exactly `["low","medium","high"]` and `defaultLevel` `"high"`; neither lists `minimal` or
  `xhigh`; both have `maxOutputTokens` 65536. The two image models (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`, `gemini-3-pro-image`) and the
  `anthropic` provider entries are unchanged.
- **AC-2:** With provider `gemini` selected in the Deep slot, and again in the Fast slot, the
  model dropdown lists exactly four entries — Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 4
  Hydrogen, Gemini 3.8 Flash — in that order, and neither "Gemini 3.7 Flash" nor "Gemini 3.6
  Flash" appears.
- **AC-3:** The cost/speed badge shown beside the selected model is `$$$` for Gemini 4 Carbon
  and Gemini 3.1 Pro, `$$ ⚡` for Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and `$ ⚡` for Gemini 3.8 Flash. The badge
  for every Anthropic model is the same as before this Story. Gemini 4 Hydrogen's catalog
  `tier` is `balanced` (`ModelSpec.tier` is `'premium' | 'balanced' | 'fast'`, in both places
  the type is declared), and its badge uses a sky colour class set, with light and dark
  variants, that differs from both the premium (amber) and the fast (emerald) sets.
- **AC-4:** The `minimal` level is not offered in the thinking-level control for Gemini 4
  Carbon, Gemini 4 Hydrogen or Gemini 3.8 Flash. Selecting Gemini 4 Carbon in a slot sets that
  slot's level to `medium`; selecting Gemini 4 Hydrogen sets it to `high`.
  `clampLevel('gemini', 'gemini-4-carbon' | 'gemini-4-hydrogen' | 'gemini-3.8-flash', 'minimal')`
  returns `low`.
- **AC-5:** Restoring stored settings in which the Deep slot or the Fast slot (each tested
  separately) is `gemini-3.7-flash` or `gemini-3.6-flash` yields that slot as provider
  `gemini` / model `gemini-3.8-flash`, with a level that is a member of
  `gemini-3.8-flash`'s `levels` (a stored `minimal` becomes `low`; a stored `low`, `medium` or
  `high` is kept). The corrected settings are persisted, so a second restore performs no
  migration. Restoring does not throw and never leaves a model id that is absent from the
  catalog. The existing `claude-sonnet-4-6` → `claude-sonnet-5-5` migration still works.
- **AC-6:** With no stored settings, and after "Reset to defaults", the Deep slot is
  `anthropic` / `claude-sonnet-5-5` at `high` and the Fast slot is `gemini` /
  `gemini-3.8-flash` at `low`; both models are in the catalog and each level is a member of its
  model's `levels`.
- **AC-7:** Switching the Deep slot's provider to `gemini` selects `gemini-4-carbon` at level
  `medium`. Switching the Fast slot's provider to `gemini` selects `gemini-3.8-flash`; adding
  the third tier does not move the Fast pick onto Gemini 4 Carbon or Gemini 4 Hydrogen.
- **AC-8:** A request the Gemini provider builds for `gemini-4-carbon` and for
  `gemini-4-hydrogen`, at each level in the model's `levels`, has `thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel`
  equal to that level, contains no `cachedContent` / `cached_content` reference, and contains
  no `temperature`, `topP` or `topK` field.
- **AC-9:** `getPrices('gemini-4-hydrogen')` returns `{ in: 1.50, out: 7.50, cw: 0, cr: 0.075 }`
  for every date. `getPrices('gemini-4-carbon')` returns `{ in: 2.00, out: 10.00, cw: 0,
  cr: 0.10 }` for any instant before `2026-12-01T00:00:00Z` and `{ in: 4.00, out: 20.00, cw: 0,
  cr: 0.20 }` from that instant on, evaluated per lookup (not at module load). Neither returns
  `FALLBACK_PRICE`, and neither resolves to another Gemini model's entry.
- **AC-10:** `getPrices('gemini-3.8-flash')` and `getPrices('gemini-3.1-pro-preview')` return
  the same values as before this Story. `server/usage/pricing.js` has no entry or helper for
  `gemini-3.6-flash` or `gemini-3.7-flash`.
- **AC-11:** `README.md`, `.env.example` and the other active (non-archived) docs and sources
  do not name `gemini-3.7-flash`, `gemini-3.6-flash`, "Gemini 3.7 Flash" or "Gemini 3.6 Flash"
  as a selectable or default model, and the existing specs that referenced them are updated to
  the new catalog.
- **AC-12:** `resolveSlot('gemini', { model: 'gemini-3.7-flash' | 'gemini-3.6-flash', level }, tier)`
  returns model `gemini-3.8-flash` for both `deep` and `fast` tiers, with the level clamped to a
  member of that model's `levels` and `maxOutputTokens` 65536, and logs one warning naming the
  retired id. A model id that is not a retired id and is not in the catalog still falls back by
  tier exactly as before.
- **AC-13:** `package.json` requires `@google/genai` as `^2.32.0` (at least 2.32.0, below
  3.0.0) and `package-lock.json` resolves it to a `2.x` version `>= 2.32.0`; no `3.x` is
  installed. The Gemini provider's calls for
  `gemini-4-carbon` and `gemini-4-hydrogen` stay non-streaming `generateContent` calls carrying
  `maxOutputTokens` 65536; no streaming call is introduced. Every pre-existing Gemini provider,
  retry and Image Studio spec that imports the SDK passes against the upgraded SDK.
- **AC-14:** `GeminiProvider`'s response reader treats `finishReason === 'CONTINUATION'` as an
  unfinished generation: it throws an error naming the model, as it does for `MAX_TOKENS`, and
  never returns the partial text.
- **AC-15:** `npm test` (both runners), `npm run lint`, `npm run build` and `bash arch-guard.sh`
  pass.

## Out of scope

- `xhigh` thinking level for Gemini 4 Hydrogen (owner chose not to register it).
- Changing the default Deep or Fast model, or the Anthropic models.
- Explicit context caching (`cachedContents`) and any cache-storage billing.
- Batch, Flex, Priority and Contributor-tier pricing; Vertex AI regional (+10%) pricing; the
  Google Search / Maps grounding fee.
- The Gemini 3.1 Pro >200K-token price tier (already noted as unmodeled in `pricing.js`).
- Re-pricing past `usage_log` rows; cost stays as computed at insert time.
- Changing prompts, schemas or generated HTML; no FROZEN file is touched.
- Live-API verification against Google; acceptance is by unit tests with mocked SDKs.
- Moving the Gemini provider to streaming calls.

## Open questions

Q1–Q6 were answered by the owner on 2026-11-15 and are recorded here with their decisions so
`so-clarifier` can carry them into the Open Decisions log; their effect is already written into
the Scope and AC-1, AC-3, AC-7, AC-9 and AC-12 above. Q7 and Q8 were raised afterwards and answered by
the owner on 2026-11-15; Q9 is new.

- **Q1 — RESOLVED:** ids `gemini-4-carbon` / `gemini-4-hydrogen`, the prices, the 1,048,576-token
  unified context, `minimal` unsupported (HTTP 400), implicit caching only
  (`cachedContents` returns HTTP 400), and `temperature` / `top_p` / `top_k` deprecated and
  ignored are confirmed by the owner against `ai.google.dev` as of 2026-11-15.
  - Residual: the confirmation came from the owner; no vendor URL is recorded in this repo yet.
- **Q2 — RESOLVED:** `maxOutputTokens` is `65536` for both new catalog entries (Google's
  documented default for synchronous requests).
- **Q3 — RESOLVED:** keep `pick(provider, 'premium')` unchanged. With Carbon first in the
  catalog, switching the Deep slot to `gemini` lands on `gemini-4-carbon` at `medium` (AC-7).
  `defaultModel('gemini')` (`models[0]`) also becomes Carbon.
- **Q4 — RESOLVED:** `resolveSlot()` in `server/providers/model-support.js` remaps
  `gemini-3.7-flash` and `gemini-3.6-flash` to `gemini-3.8-flash` with a warning (AC-12).
  - Checked: `resolveSlot()` already degrades any unknown id to the first model of the slot's
    tier. Without the remap a stale Deep-slot request would land on Carbon (first premium), not
    on a Flash model; a stale Fast-slot request would already land on `gemini-3.8-flash`. The
    remap makes both deterministic. No `GEMINI_MODEL_*` environment override exists in `server/`,
    `src/` or the README, so that case does not arise.
- **Q5 — RESOLVED:** tier name `balanced`; Hydrogen's `$$ ⚡` badge is sky/cyan, distinct from
  amber (`$$$`) and emerald (`$ ⚡`) (AC-3). The owner's example classes were dark-theme only;
  this Story requires light and dark variants, as the existing badges have.
- **Q6 — RESOLVED:** the Carbon price switches at `2026-12-01T00:00:00Z` (UTC), consistent with
  the `2027-01-01T00:00:00Z` boundary for `gemini-3.8-flash`. Google's pages give calendar dates
  only; if billing flips later in Pacific time the app shows the new rate for up to ~8 hours
  early (the conservative direction).
- **Q7 — RESOLVED:** upgrade `@google/genai` to `^2.32.0` (AC-13); keep `maxOutputTokens`
  65536 for `gemini-4-carbon` and stay on non-streaming calls. The owner reports that from
  `@google/genai >= 2.29.1` (full long-decode support from `2.32.0`) `automatic_continuation`
  is on by default, so an SDK-level second request stitches a Carbon response past the 32,768
  token single-hop limit; below that version the response stops at 32,768 with
  `finishReason: CONTINUATION`. These version and behaviour claims are the owner's and were not
  verified against Google's pages when recorded.
  - Checked: installed `@google/genai` is `1.52.0` (`package.json`: `^1.43.0`), a major-version
    upgrade away from the target. The owner also reports that `3.0.0` (2026-11-06) and `3.2.0`
    (2026-11-12) are published, with Node `>= 22` and a default API version change from `v1beta`
    to `v1`; the range is therefore capped at `^2.32.0`. The repo's Node is `24.22.2`
    (`.nvmrc`, `engines`), so the Node floor is not the constraint. The SDK is used by `server/providers/gemini.js`,
    `server/utils/retry.js`, `server/utils/timeouts.js` and the Gemini, retry and Image Studio
    specs, so the upgrade reaches Image Studio too.
- **Q8 — RESOLVED:** `#readText()` in `server/providers/gemini.js` throws on
  `finishReason === 'CONTINUATION'` (fail-closed, like `MAX_TOKENS`) so a truncated artifact
  never reaches the validator (AC-14). The owner reports the SDK normally consumes `CONTINUATION`
  itself on `>= 2.32.0`, so this is a guard for the abnormal case (continuation disabled, missing
  token, API behaviour change).
- **Q9:** `generateContent()` on `@google/genai >= 2.32.0` can issue two sequential HTTP
  requests for one call when continuation fires. Do the per-call `httpOptions.timeout` values
  (`DEEP_TIMEOUT_MS` 1,200,000 ms, `VISION_TIMEOUT_MS` 600,000 ms, `FAST_TIMEOUT_MS`
  120,000 ms in `server/utils/timeouts.js`) apply to each HTTP request or to the whole call?
  - Checked: the values and their call sites in `gemini.js`; the SDK's timeout semantics under
    continuation were not exercised, and the owner's note on this is a request to check, not a
    finding.
  - Impact if unresolved: a Carbon call that needs a second hop could hit the Fast timeout
    (120 s) or the proxy timeout even though each hop is within budget; the spec writer would
    have to assume which.
