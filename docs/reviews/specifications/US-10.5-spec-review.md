## Non-blocking findings

- **N-A (FR-17 wording; fix at the next edit, not a blocker).** The "A v4 change to an approved
  block" paragraph reads "while a generation is running (or an attachment is uploading, or the
  session is blocked) and the draft is blank with nothing attached". Under rule 3 the label applies
  when the composer holds "no ready upload and no attached version", so a blank draft with an upload
  still in progress (not ready) reads "Write a prompt to start" even though something is attached.
  "Nothing attached" in the paragraph contradicts that case; the normative rule 3 should prevail and
  the paragraph should say "no ready upload and no attached version". A test author who reads the
  paragraph could expect "Generate" for blank draft plus upload in progress.
- **N-B (small unlisted moves, all supported by Story v5).** (1) FR-24: the sentence "A Retry restarts
  the clock ..." moved from the v3 Proposed block (OD-3c) to the base text, and "Both are recorded
  when the turn is stored" became "in the stored turn". Story v5 AC-13a ("from the 'Send' or 'Retry'
  click") and the ruling's ratification text support the Retry start, but revision row 9 does not
  name the promotion. (2) FR-32 OD-20 Proposed block: opening and closing sentences were reworded
  ("Story v5 added the two changes now carried by clause (d)"; "outside (a)-(d) and this list"),
  and the §7.7 sentence moved from the SQ-5 block to the base failure path. Both are consequences of
  the rulings. The Background statement "everything not named in the Revision notes ... is unchanged"
  is true at section level.
- **N-C (traceability label).** Story v5 AC-15 refers to "the Specification's table SQ-5"; v4 has
  no SQ-5 heading (it is "table D-1..D-12; approved ... with the ruling SQ-5-B, OD-33"). A
  reconciliation reader maps them through the ruling id; consider naming both in the table heading.
- **N-D (Background attribution).** Background says Story v5 Context adds "the 'Write a prompt to
  start' label that follows the draft". Story v5 does not say that; it is the Specification's reading
  of AC-11 and reviewer note N2(b), disclosed in FR-17. Wording only.
- **N-E (apparent tension, inherited from v3).** Out of scope says "Deleting the code or specs behind
  the removed download-format selector (FR-6, OD-24)" while D-5 removes the component-level
  "Download format" selector tests. FR-6 Proposed scopes the "left as they are" list to the
  module-level code and specs (`output-format.spec.ts`, `zip.spec.ts`, `inputFormat`, `downloaded`).
  Owner-approved table D-5 prevails; consider saying "module-level" in Out of scope.
- **N-F (carried, not new).** Owner dependencies that remain Proposed and need the human at the
  gate: OD-27, OD-28, the OD-15 normalisation order and transliteration scheme, OD-13 remainder, OD-20
  remainder (including whether the `PRN-*` copy is kept in step), OD-21 (hand-computed contrast
  figures not tool-verified), OD-5 UA copy, OD-35 detach-before-send. None is a blocker.
- **N-H (start instant of `durationMs`, inherited from Story AC-13a).** FR-24 and Story AC-13a start
  the clock at "the Send click (or the Retry click)". A Ctrl+Enter submit (FR-17 rule 1 makes it
  equivalent to a click) and a quick-edit chip send also store a turn, and neither has a stated start

  whatever triggered it. Not new to v4 and not a blocker; record it for TEST_WRITING.

  approval the human should be shown the Open questions table rows marked Proposed and the two OPEN
