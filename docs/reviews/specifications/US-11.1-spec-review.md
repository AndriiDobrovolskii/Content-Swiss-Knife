## AC-13 against FR-22 as scoped (the question the approver is asked to confirm)

Story AC-13, verbatim: "The Gemini provider's calls for `gemini-4-carbon` and `gemini-4-hydrogen`
stay non-streaming `generateContent` calls carrying `maxOutputTokens` 65536; no streaming call is
introduced." FR-22 v2 binds 65536 only on the `generate()` path and states that `analyzeImage` and
`extractFromPdf` keep their caps.

What I verified in `server/providers/gemini.js`:

- `generate()` passes the resolved slot's `maxOutputTokens` (65536 for both models); no streaming call
  exists anywhere in the file (no `stream` hit).
- `analyzeImage` passes `Math.min(8000, maxOutputTokens)` (line 250), which is 8000 for both models.
- `extractFromPdf` passes `8192` (line 272).

So the literal AC-13 sentence is false of the unchanged code on two of the three paths, and the word
"stay" shows the Story author believed every call already carried 65536. The Story's own text points
the other way on how to resolve it: Q7 says "keep `maxOutputTokens` 65536 for `gemini-4-carbon` and
stay on non-streaming calls" (a statement about long text generation and the single-hop limit), the
Story Surface lists `gemini.js` as "verify only (request shape for the new ids)", and no AC, Open
Decision or Out of scope line asks for the vision and PDF caps to rise.

Verdict on this item: **non-blocking, for the human to confirm; not a CLARIFICATION loop-back.**

1. It is a narrowing of an AC's literal text, which a Specification is not normally free to do. But it
   is not silent: FR-22, Out of scope, the traceability row, N-3 and the Revision history all state
   it, and N-3 asks the approver to confirm it. The rule against "silent rewording of scope" is what
   is protected here, and nothing is silent.
2. The literal reading cannot be satisfied without a change nobody asked for (raising the deliberate
   caps for a short caption and a mechanical transcription, with the cost and truncation risk the
   plan review records as K-5/F-3). Choosing between "narrow the clause" and "raise the caps" is an
   owner scope decision. The reviewer cannot make it, and the Specification correctly did not.
3. Looping back would not improve the document. A CLARIFICATION loop would ask the owner the same
   question HUMAN_SPEC_APPROVAL already asks, with a third pass of the pipeline in between. The
   Story is not untestable at source: AC-13's other clauses (range, lock, no 3.x, non-streaming,
   existing specs pass) are fully testable, and the 65536 clause is testable on the path the owner's
   Q7 is about.
4. The in-scope behaviour is bound with a failure path (FR-22) on all three provider paths, including a
   guard that the two lower caps do not change, so the narrowing cannot hide a regression.

Residual risk, and what the approver should do about it: the Story text is not edited, so a later
reader (the reconciliation reviewer in particular) who compares tests to the Story's AC-13 verbatim
will see an unasserted 65536 on `analyzeImage` and `extractFromPdf`. The approver should therefore
state the reading in the approval note for Specification v2 (for example: "AC-13's 65536 clause
applies to the `generate()` text path; the image-caption and PDF caps are unchanged") so that
`history.jsonl` carries it, as the OD rulings already do. If the owner instead wants those caps
raised, that is new scope and a Specification revision, not a reading.

## Verdict rationale

`PASS`, not `CHANGES_REQUIRED`: the only substantive change in v2 is the scoping of FR-22, which
resolves plan-review B-1 as that finding recommended (its first alternative), is consistent with the
unchanged code I read, and is disclosed everywhere it matters. The remaining difference between FR-22
and the Story's AC-13 text is a decision the owner, not a reviewer, makes, and the approval gate asks
it by name (N-3). All six axes are clear apart from the non-blocking items below. No other FR, NFR,
Proposed block or Open question changed, and the v1 review's findings stand.

Not `BLOCKED`: all three inputs are current `DRAFT` versions and no Open Decision is blocking.

A `changes_required` -> `SPECIFICATION` outcome would be warranted only if the approver rules that
the 65536 clause must hold on every provider path; that is a post-approval-gate outcome, not a defect
in the document as written.

  record them as such rather than as a TDD miss. If the Carbon and Hydrogen catalog entries (FR-2,
  FR-3) are not yet present, the `generate()` 65536 test for those ids does fail first, which is the
