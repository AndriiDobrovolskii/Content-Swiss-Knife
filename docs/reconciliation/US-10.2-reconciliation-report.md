   remained, as the Specification (FR-22) and the evidence both say. The gate stage's four further full logic runs and the T5 pre-series runs showed no marker either; they are
   not extra measurements for AC-14.
2. **Series A failed and is not counted; the human decided that.** In the first ten-run attempt runs 1-5 were clean and run 6 failed (exit 1, `Tests 3 failed`, all three
   in `test/measure-description.cli.spec.ts`, `Test timed out in 5000ms`, worker-exit markers 0). The pipeline status diagnoses it as a load-sensitive timeout; that is a
   **hypothesis, not proved**, and what loaded the machine was not identified. Reading FR-22 and OD-17 literally, "a run that fails for another reason ... still counts as a run"
   and "there is no retry-until-clean". The human then authorized a fresh series B on the unchanged tree (`HUMAN_DECISION` 2026-11-03T15:45:15Z, option a). Series B is
   clean and the evidence names series A. I record this as a human-authorized departure from the literal text, not as a defect, and flag it for the gate: the claim must stay
   "series B, ten runs, no crash observed, after a series A that failed on a non-worker timeout", never "ten of ten clean". The 5000 ms timeout in an unrelated file is an
   intermittent gate-reliability observation this Story neither caused nor fixed.
3. **AC-10 and AC-11 are recorded data.** The live upload was run by the builder (`so-builder`) at the human's decisions (`T2.runner=builder` 2026-11-03T12:43:58Z; `T3.runner=builder`
   2026-11-03T15:10:47Z), not by the human (the Specification's default, OD-7, accepted the builder-with-`.env` alternative). The upload ran at HEAD `c7c0a76`; the pair fingerprint then
