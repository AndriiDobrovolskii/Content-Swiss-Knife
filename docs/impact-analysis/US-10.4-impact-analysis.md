## What the Specification asks for, in blast-radius terms

A pure Angular-track change to the app shell. One component template (`src/app/app.component.html`, 1927 lines, 128 548 bytes) is restructured: the in-panel mode switcher and the panel header controls are replaced by a global rail; the `<aside>` input panel becomes conditional on mode; Clear All, the improvement-request block and the History button/overlay move or are gated with the panel; the root, `<main>` and panel surfaces are re-coloured with dark tokens plus a derived light theme. No prompt, schema, renderer, validator, store, locale, server route or fixture is touched. No FROZEN file is touched.

## FR-16 / OD-29: width at 1024 px, from markup (not measured)

### What constrains width (derived from the markup)

- `<body>` is `h-screen overflow-hidden` (`index.html` L33). `app-root` content root is `flex h-screen w-full` (`app.component.html` L2). Nothing scrolls at page level; anything wider than its box is either scrolled by an inner container or clipped.
- Panel today: `w-96 xl:w-[26rem] flex-shrink-0` (L5). `xl` is 1280 px, so at 1024 px the panel is `w-96` = 384 px (the Specification's figure is right).
- `<main>` is `flex-1 flex flex-col h-full ... relative overflow-hidden` (L1085). Because `overflow` is not `visible`, its automatic minimum width is 0, so it shrinks to the remainder and **clips** anything wider than that remainder that is not in a scrolling child. No `min-w-[...]`/`min-w-<n>` utility exists in `<main>`; the only two `min-w-` hits in the file are `min-w-0`, at L369 (inside the panel) and L1886 (image-tools result row in `<main>`).
- The rail (new) would need `flex-shrink-0` like the panel; otherwise at 1024 px it can shrink. That is a design detail, recorded here because it changes the arithmetic.

### Width arithmetic at a 1024 px viewport (rail and panel as specified; borders inside the box under Tailwind preflight)

| Mode group | Rail expanded 256 | Rail collapsed 64 | Today (no rail, 384 px panel in every mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 panel modes (9 text + image-tools) | main = 1024 - 256 - 384 = **384 px** | main = 1024 - 64 - 384 = **576 px** | 640 px |
| 3 panel-less modes (image-studio, html-editor, dashboard) | main = 1024 - 256 = **768 px** | main = 1024 - 64 = **960 px** | 640 px |

So the OD-29 shortfall exists **only in the ten panel modes with an expanded rail** (384 vs 640 px); in the collapsed rail the panel modes have 576 px (64 px less than today); the three panel-less modes get **more** width than today at both rail states. FR-16's expanded-rail cell is therefore the one in doubt for 10 of the 13 modes.

### Fixed or viewport-keyed widths that react badly to a narrow main (derived)

- **Viewport-keyed breakpoints do not see the narrowed main.** At 1024 px `md:` (768) and `lg:` (1024, inclusive) are both active whatever `<main>` width is: `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2` at L1230 (readability result), L1576 (SEO panel), L1672 (slug panel); `grid-cols-2 md:grid-cols-4 lg:grid-cols-6` at L1846 (Image Tools thumbnails). At a 384 px main with `p-8` (32 px each side) that is 320 px of content: the thumbnail grid is 6 columns with `gap-4` (16 px), roughly 40 px per tile, with 24 px absolutely positioned remove buttons (`-top-2 -right-2 w-6 h-6`, button at L1849) on top; the SEO and slug grids become two columns of roughly 150 px with `pr-8` copy controls. These are legible-or-cramped, not unreachable; whether they pass "usable" is a judgement only a render can settle.
- **The result tab strip.** L1309 `flex items-center gap-1 overflow-x-auto no-scrollbar` holds `whitespace-nowrap` tab buttons (`pb-4 px-2`) generated per store language, per FAQ artifact, plus SEO/Slugs. The registry has stores with 5 languages (`Center 3D Print`: pl-PL, en-GB, de-DE, uk-UA, ru-UA), so a fully generated run has up to roughly eleven tabs (master + translations + FAQ per language + SEO). The class `no-scrollbar` is **not defined anywhere** (grep over `src` and `index.html` finds only the usage), so the strip simply scrolls horizontally with the default 8 px scrollbar. It is reachable, not clipped. But FR-16 literally says "no horizontal scrolling is required to reach anything"; this strip almost certainly already needs horizontal scrolling at today's 640 px for the larger stores and will at 384 px for almost any store with two languages and a FAQ. Whether "required to reach" counts a deliberate in-component scroll container is not stated in the Specification. See Unknowns U-1.
- **Code panes.** The five `<pre class="... overflow-x-auto ... whitespace-pre-wrap">` blocks (L1494, 1522, 1567, 1748, 1792) wrap text and scroll inside themselves; they cannot be clipped.
- **Generated-HTML previews** (`[innerHTML]` cards with `prose ... max-w-none p-8`): the content is arbitrary product HTML (spec tables, figures). The scroll container ancestors are `overflow-y-auto`, which makes `overflow-x` compute to `auto` as well, so a table wider than the card scrolls inside the main pane. Reachable, but "horizontal scroll required" in the strict reading, as today at 640 px.
- **Panel-less interiors** (all wider than today): `dashboard` is `absolute inset-0 overflow-y-auto`, its tables sit in `overflow-x-auto` (dashboard html L151), stat grids use `md:grid-cols-5`/`md:grid-cols-3`; `html-editor` is `absolute inset-0 overflow-y-auto` with `max-w-4xl mx-auto` content (flex-wrap toolbar, popovers `w-64`/`w-80 max-w-[calc(100%-1rem)]`); `image-studio` is `lg:flex-row` with a `lg:w-64 flex-shrink-0` session list and `flex-1 min-w-0` content, root `overflow-auto`, i.e. 768 - 32 - 256 - 16 = 464 px (expanded) or 656 px (collapsed) for the content column, against 336 px today. None has a fixed minimum width that exceeds 768 px.
- **Comparison modal** (L1146) is `fixed inset-0`, viewport-based, and unaffected by the main width.

### Verdict on FR-16 from the repo alone

FR-16 is **not provably unreachable**: in every mode the main area scrolls vertically (`overflow-y-auto` on each view) and nothing in `<main>` has a fixed width above 384 px that would be cut off by `overflow-hidden`. It is **not provably satisfiable either**: the literal clause "no horizontal scrolling is required" is at best contestable for the generator result tab strip and wide generated tables, in the expanded-rail cell of the ten panel modes. The Specification already routes a failure that needs a panel-width or interior change to a Story amendment; that route stays correct.

### What could not be measured without running the app

- Actual rendered widths (font metrics, scrollbar widths, `p-6`/`p-8` interplay) of the tab strip, SEO/slug cards, thumbnail tiles at 384 px and 576 px.
- How many tabs a real generation produces for a 5-language store and whether they exceed 384, 576 and today's 640 px (needs a run with a result, or a fixture-fed component test in a real browser; happy-dom has no layout engine).
- Whether any tooltip/popover positioned `absolute` (for example the `w-48`/`w-56` hover tooltips at L1594-1644, the html-editor `w-64` popovers) overflows the main edge in a narrow main.
- Whether the new rail's own contents (12 items + 3 groups + 4 bottom controls) fit a 768 px viewport height; no height constraint is in the Specification, and `h-screen` + `overflow-hidden` on `body` would clip a rail that does not scroll internally. At 1024 px width the common heights are not specified; this is a second clip risk the Specification does not mention (see Unknowns U-3).
- Contrast, colour and token rendering (FR-13..FR-15): Tailwind is the CDN, and the unit runners do not load it (Specification Out of scope), so none of it can be asserted automatically.

## Silent-failure risks (produce no error, only a wrong or vacuous result)

1. **`aside(h)` returns the rail.** If the rail is an `<aside>` placed before the panel, `clear-all` L52 selects it. L78 (`toHaveLength(0)` for `input[type=range]`) then passes vacuously and L90's `> 0` fails. Conversely a rail given `role="complementary"` is invisible to this spec but still distinguishable by other queries. Either way a green result in L78 is only meaningful if the helper provably finds the panel.
2. **Vacuous absence pins.** Any "no Process Images / no range control in studio mode" assertion written as "within the panel" passes trivially once the panel is gone; OD-30 row 3 handles the existing one, but the planner should make every absence check non-vacuous by also asserting the panel absence itself.
3. **`generatorForm` template reference scope.** `<ng-template #generatorForm>` is declared inside the `<aside>` (L190) and consumed twice: at L421 (inside the aside) and at **L1299 in `<main>`** (`*ngTemplateOutlet="generatorForm"`), the pre-result centred form for generator and ua-generator. A template reference declared inside an `@if (...) { }` block is not visible outside that block in Angular's control-flow scoping. Wrapping the `<aside>` in a gate while leaving the `ng-template` inside it would break L1299: the failure mode is a template compile error or an `undefined` outlet, and the generator and ua-generator pre-result form would not render in the main area (the panel in those modes would still render, which is why a quick check of panel presence would not notice). The template has to be reachable from both places. (Recorded as impact; the shape of the fix is the planner's.)
4. **Indentation-sensitive source pins** (`faq-layout` `formBlock`, 4 spaces) go red if the panel is re-nested by a formatter, or, worse, if a duplicate 4-space `@else if (appMode() === 'faq-generator')` line appears elsewhere before the real one and the pin finds the wrong block while its anchors still count out.
5. **Dashboard-first slice** in `template-wiring.switcher()`: moving Dashboard to the bottom while keeping a `<!-- History Panel` comment later in the file returns a slice with no mode buttons, so "found X in the switcher" pins fail loudly; but if a new Dashboard control also remained near the top (a duplicate `setMode('dashboard')`), the slice would silently be a different, wrong region and the FAQ-beside-UA pin could pass on the wrong text.
6. **Double accessible name.** FR-6 requires unique names among rail buttons. A collapsed rail that keeps a visually hidden label plus an `aria-label` or `title` on the same element is fine; two copies of the item (one for the expanded, one for the icon-only layout both rendered, one `hidden` by CSS) would produce two role matches in a happy-dom/Chromium component test only if `hidden` is not exposed as an accessibility-tree exclusion. In the component runner Tailwind classes are not applied, so a CSS-only `hidden` does nothing and both copies are found: tests would see two buttons that a real browser hides.
7. **CSS-only hiding is invisible to the component runner.** Because Tailwind is the Play CDN and not loaded in `ng test`, any behaviour done by classes alone (`hidden`, `w-16` icon-only label hiding, `dark:` colours, truncation) cannot be asserted by the unit tests and cannot regress there; this is what the Specification's manual FR-19 matrix is for. A requirement implemented as classes (FR-2 (b): hidden items not focusable and not exposed) needs a template-level `@if`, or the FR-2 (b) test is vacuous.
8. **Opaque root layer.** `<div class="flex h-screen ... bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900">` (L2) sits between `<body>` and `<main>`, and `<main>` is `bg-slate-50/50 dark:bg-slate-900/50`. A page background set only on `<body>` or `<main>` is covered or tinted; FR-13 warns of this. No automated test can see it.
9. **Persisted-key shape.** FR-5's new key is read with `JSON.parse` semantics in the neighbouring code (`seo_gen_dark_mode`, L662-665, which is unguarded and would throw on a malformed value in the **constructor**). The new key is specified as the raw strings `true`/`false` with a guarded read; copying the dark-mode pattern would be a silent regression of FR-5 (ii).
10. **Feedback block gating by accident.** FR-17 is satisfied for free if the whole Actions Footer (which contains the feedback block, L1057-1080) stays inside the panel; it is violated if the block is lifted out of the gated region while moving markup. `README.md` L127 describes the same control.
11. **Hidden fallback in the footer chain.** The Actions Footer's final `@else { <button (click)="processImages()" ...` (the branch right after the `image-studio` one) is the branch that leaks Process Images in html-editor and dashboard today. Gating the panel removes that leak at the panel level only; the branch itself stays (OD-31), so a future change that renders the footer without the gate re-leaks it silently.
12. **`confirm()` in `setMode`.** The unsaved-HTML-editor guard returns early without changing the mode; a rail that updates its own "active" state before calling `setMode` (instead of reading `appMode()`) would show a wrong `aria-current` after a cancelled confirm. FR-3 counts `aria-current` and would not notice.

## Things that would error loudly (for contrast)

Missing `TRANSLATIONS` key in one language (the typed `uiLabels()` access is checked by `tsc --noEmit`/`npm run lint` if keys are accessed as properties of the shared object type); a template reference to a removed method; `faq-layout` and `template-wiring` premise guards (they fail with named messages); `ng build` template type-check errors.

## Blast-radius summary

US-10.4 is confined to the Angular shell: `app.component.html` (restructure and re-colour, about a thousand-line panel gated, a 90-line switcher and header block replaced), `app.component.ts` (rail and group state, a guarded `localStorage` key, new keys in both `TRANSLATIONS` maps), `index.html`/`src/styles.css` (tokens and light theme), `src/app/types.ts`, and new rail/panel components with their own specs under the `ng test` runner. No FROZEN file, store, locale, prompt, schema, renderer, validator, server file or corpus fixture is reached, and no §4 criterion is in play. The real risk is test-side and layout-side. Test-side: four existing specs read `app.component.html` as text or select `aside`; the FAQ and image-studio mode-button literals, the Dashboard-to-History slice, the four-space `@else if` indentation, the Actions Footer anchor and the `aside` helper are all brittle, and the Actions Footer must stay in `app.component.html` for `image-studio-wiring` to pass unchanged. The panel gate must also keep `<ng-template #generatorForm>` reachable from `<main>`. Layout-side: at 1024 px the ten panel modes have 384 px of main with the expanded rail (576 px collapsed, 640 px today), the three panel-less modes have 768 px/960 px (wider than today), `md:`/`lg:` grid breakpoints are keyed to the viewport and so ignore the narrower main, and the generated-result tab strip already scrolls horizontally. FR-16 is neither provably unreachable nor provable from the repo; the design-time measurement OD-29 assigns to ARCHITECTURE_PLANNING must be done in a real browser, and the expanded-rail panel-mode cell is where it is most likely to fail.

## Unknowns

- **U-1. Does a deliberate in-component horizontal scroll (result tab strip, wide generated tables) violate FR-16's "no horizontal scrolling is required to reach anything"?** The Specification does not say. The tab strip needs horizontal scrolling at today's 640 px for the larger stores, so under the strict reading FR-16 cannot be met at 384 px (or perhaps at all, for multi-language stores) without changing a mode interior, which is out of scope. Settle: a product-owner reading of the clause (strict or "not clipped, scrollable inside its own region is fine"), or a Story amendment. This does not block planning, and the verdict below is PASS on the basis that the Specification already routes such a failure to a Story amendment; it is flagged so ARCHITECTURE_PLANNING measures early (OD-29) and does not discover it at manual verification.
- **U-2. Real rendered widths and tab counts** in the cells listed under "What could not be measured". Settle: run the app (or a Chromium-based component harness) at 1024 x 768 with a seeded generation result for a 5-language store, both rail states, all 13 modes; record the numbers in the FR-19 evidence artifact.
- **U-3. Rail vertical fit.** The Specification gives widths but no height requirement; 12 items + 3 headers + 4 bottom controls + logo is about 20 rows on a body that is `h-screen overflow-hidden`. Whether the rail needs its own vertical scroll at common laptop heights is unspecified and not measurable here.
- **U-4. OD-31 vs OD-32 placement.** OD-31 fixes `image-studio-wiring` as unchanged (which pins the Actions Footer branch to `app.component.html`); OD-32 allows repointing pins when markup moves. The Specification states both without saying which wins for a relocated Actions Footer. Settle at ARCHITECTURE_PLANNING by keeping the footer in `app.component.html`, or by an explicit human decision.
- **U-5. Whether the rail is an `<aside>`/`<nav>`/`<div>`** and its DOM order relative to the panel, which decides the `aside(h)` hazard. Design choice for the planner; recorded here as an impact, not decided.
- **U-6. Count of existing-spec edits.** The exact set of `template-wiring` / `faq-layout` / `clear-all` edits depends on the markup split (OD-32) and cannot be fixed before ARCHITECTURE_PLANNING; the list above is the superset the survey found.
